# Third-party notices

## Hershey fonts

`lib/render/fonts/serif.json` and `lib/render/fonts/script.json` are converted from
the "Hershey Serif Medium" and "Hershey Script 1" SVG fonts distributed in the
[`hersheytext`](https://github.com/techninja/hersheytextjs) package (MIT), prepared by
Windell H. Oskay (Evil Mad Scientist) from emergent.unpythonic.net/software/hershey.

The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the
U. S. National Bureau of Standards.

The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by
James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821
(mit-eddie!ci-dandelion!hurt).
